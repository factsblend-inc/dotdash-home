The details behind your plan.

Branches and account eligibility

Entry is limited to one branch and one account per legal entity or tax ID. Upgrade to Growth for a second branch. Growth includes two branches; Pro includes four. Every additional branch costs ฿990/month. All branches in an account share menus, points, and customer data.

Delivery providers

Growth and Pro coordinate multiple providers to select the fastest and most cost-effective option for each order. Start with GrabExpress and Lalamove. Skootar is coming next. Pro lists Own Fleet.

CRM, members, and LINE messaging

Basic and Advanced CRM are priced per account, not per branch. Member counts and points administrators are unlimited. Existing DotDash members can join your store in one click, under fair-use terms. LINE broadcast quotas follow your LINE OA plan; DotDash adds no message fee.

AI credits

Advanced CRM includes 1,000 credits/month, shared across branches. Credits reset each month and do not roll over. Extra packs cost ฿290 per 1,000 credits.

Existing POS and Meri PoS

Online orders connect to supported existing POS systems with no additional integration fee; ask the team which systems are supported. Meri PoS is ฿250/branch/month with an ordering plan, normally ฿500. Stock and Crew require Meri PoS.

Your branded app

Entry/Growth: ฿1,490/account/month plus ฿25,000 setup. Pro: included, with no setup charge. Apps are published under the partner’s Apple and Google developer accounts, paid for by the partner. Standard design is adapted to your brand; custom features are quoted separately. Upgrading to Pro carries the app over with no new setup fee or monthly app charge.

Annual billing

Save 10% on all recurring monthly services. One-time app setup and payment fees are excluded. Annual billing is available only with fixed monthly pricing.

Capped usage billing and payment fees

All three plans offer 10% of online order value, capped at the plan price for the selected branch count, or fixed monthly pricing. Add-ons remain separate recurring charges. This option has no announced end date; changes require at least 60 days’ notice. Payment rates: fixed 0.35%+ per bill; capped usage 1.65%+ per bill. BYO payment is available on Pro; confirm applicable terms with the team.

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I have to stop using marketplaces?

No. Keep using them where they bring useful demand. DotDash gives your existing customers a direct channel and helps your brand build relationships and repeats.

What CRM comes with my plan?

Every plan includes Basic CRM: points, rewards, membership levels, customer profiles, automatic RFM, three active campaigns, analytics, and ROI. Advanced CRM is ฿1,290/account/month on any plan, with custom segments, ten campaigns, scheduled campaigns, targeted broadcasts, and AI.

Can we keep our existing POS?

Yes, for supported systems, with no additional integration charge. Ask the team to confirm compatibility. Or use Meri PoS at ฿250/branch/month with your ordering plan. Stock and Crew are separate Meri PoS add-ons.

How are additional branches charged?

Entry includes one branch and cannot add more. Growth includes two branches and Pro includes four. Extra Growth/Pro branches are ฿990/month each. CRM and branded-app subscriptions are per account, while Meri PoS, Stock, and Crew are per branch.

How does capped usage billing work?

Pay 10% of online order value up to your branch-adjusted plan price. Optional add-ons are charged separately. Payment fees start at 1.65%+ per bill on this model. Fixed billing has payment fees of 0.35%+ per bill; annual fixed billing saves 10% on recurring services.
